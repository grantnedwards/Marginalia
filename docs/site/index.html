Red. Worst state wins: any failed reminder, or a beat over 180s, or no beat at all. Yellow is a skipped reminder and nothing worse.

The colour is the whole command. 180s sits deliberately below watchdog.sh’s 600s, so an organizer sees the failure before the container is restarted out from under it. The heartbeat is reported even with no cycle open — process liveness is not scheduler liveness.

Setup, once

1 Invite the bot to your server
2 Turn on the Server Members intent
3 Drag its role above the cohort roles
4 /ingest a DRM-free EPUB you own — optional

Step 3 is the one people miss. Without it every role assignment fails, and Discord's error never says why.
